# Surface material notes

Both albedo maps were generated with the built-in ImageGen tool, then reduced to 512 pixels square and encoded as WebP. The runtime materials apply them in world space so that the grain retains its scale across structural members.

## Jacket steel

Full-frame seamless repeatable square game material albedo photograph. A close orthographic survey of the skin of a submerged offshore steel jacket, faded deep turquoise-grey marine coating in irregular patches, fine pale calcareous flecks and many tiny pits, short reddish brown rust streaks and subtle old scratches, lightly mottled. Flat diffuse neutral illumination, zero shadows, surface detail at a believable one metre scale, graphite and desaturated petrol green. Edge to edge single continuous planar material, no object silhouettes, no writing, no framing, no borders.

## Bell enamel

Full-frame seamless repeatable square game material albedo, close orthographic photograph of aging ochre yellow safety enamel on a North Sea work bell. Uneven mustard yellow paint, faded topside yellow underneath, small chips through to raw oxide dark metal and scratches, subtle salt accretion, authentic maritime machinery. Medium light albedo, fine scale material variation over a one metre square, flat diffuse light with no shading. Edge to edge single continuous painted material with no symbols, no seams or bolts, no labels or writing, no framing.
