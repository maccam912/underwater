# Surface material notes

Both albedo maps were generated with the built-in ImageGen tool, then reduced to 512 pixels square and encoded as WebP. The runtime materials apply them in world space so that the grain retains its scale across structural members.

## Jacket steel

Full-frame seamless repeatable square game material albedo photograph. A close orthographic survey of the skin of a submerged offshore steel jacket, faded deep turquoise-grey marine coating in irregular patches, fine pale calcareous flecks and many tiny pits, short reddish brown rust streaks and subtle old scratches, lightly mottled. Flat diffuse neutral illumination, zero shadows, surface detail at a believable one metre scale, graphite and desaturated petrol green. Edge to edge single continuous planar material, no object silhouettes, no writing, no framing, no borders.

## Bell enamel

Full-frame seamless repeatable square game material albedo, close orthographic photograph of aging ochre yellow safety enamel on a North Sea work bell. Uneven mustard yellow paint, faded topside yellow underneath, small chips through to raw oxide dark metal and scratches, subtle salt accretion, authentic maritime machinery. Medium light albedo, fine scale material variation over a one metre square, flat diffuse light with no shading. Edge to edge single continuous painted material with no symbols, no seams or bolts, no labels or writing, no framing.

## Bell clipboard

This 900-pixel square WebP is an original built-in ImageGen prop photograph. The paperwork and editable entries are drawn over it in the engine.

Game prop texture, straight-on orthographic archival photograph of an entire old square oil rig dive supervisor's clipboard. The clipboard exactly fills a square image, outside margin at most 1%, axis aligned, seen dead face-on, flat, no perspective. Rectangular subdued brown compressed-fibre clipboard backing, a thick dog-eared pad of light buff manila carbon copy paper nearly edge to edge. Small dulled dark nickel spring clip centered along the uppermost 6%, sides rough, thumb-polished edge, corners damp and fraying, oily thumb traces clustered along the extreme right and lower edge, very fine fibers, bent bottom corner and faint isolated salt rings. Centre and interior spacious, pale and largely blank for readable ink added in engine. The paper lies flat. Flat even warm-grey lighting, utilitarian object from a North Sea vessel 1991, used for years. Absolutely no text, writing, marks resembling letters, numbers, ruled lines, logos or design graphics. No surrounding scene or hands. An authentic weathered object rather than graphic design.

## Oilskin logbook

Built-in ImageGen produced the cover. The name and prompts on the cloth tape are drawn in the game.

Photorealistic game prop, orthographic overhead scan of one closed old North Sea diver's watch logbook, a roughly square landscape 6:5 pocket book. A worn dark olive-gray oilskin hard cover, thick brown thread and abraded binding at far left, rubbed-through cotton, one crush-marked corner, little greasy fingerprints. At the top third, one broad horizontal strip of blank dirty cream cloth adhesive tape extending across most of the cover, with hand-torn ends and fraying edges, large enough for the title which will be added later. Keep the middle and lower half open dark textured cloth with a little wear. The book is aligned perfectly square with the image, viewed straight-on with its entire irregular silhouette visible, tiny margin around it, near-flat lighting. Isolated on transparent background, no shadow beyond object. Absolutely no text, numbers, logos, embossing or handwriting anywhere. Real battered tool-room notebook, understated 1990s industrial feel.
